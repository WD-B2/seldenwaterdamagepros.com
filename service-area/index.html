Selden, ZIP 11784

Selden is a hamlet in the Town of Brookhaven with a 2020 Census population of 21,262 across 4.3 square miles. NY 25, also called Middle Country Road, is the main east-west road and holds most of the commercial development, so a leak in a plaza suite can reach the neighboring unit through a shared wall. If you rent a unit, tell the landlord the same day.

Blue Point Road, Boyle Road and Captain Daniel Roe Highway (CR 111) run through the residential areas. The Nature's Gardens lots, sold from 1931, lie south of Middle Country Road and Evergreen Drive, and the Telescope Hill area is on the south side. When you call, give the nearest main road and a cross street so the dispatcher can route the crew correctly.

Suburban building took off in the early 1950s, and the area has 7,167 housing units. Older houses may have original galvanized or cast iron supply lines, so a plumber should look at the whole run after a leak. A pre-1980 house may also have materials that need testing before demolition.

Mount Sinai and Middle Island have their own pages

Mount Sinai, ZIP 11766, is about 4 miles northeast of Selden on the North Shore, with a harbor inside the hamlet and Cedar Beach between the harbor and Long Island Sound. Homes near the water should check the FEMA flood map for their address, because regular homeowners policies usually exclude flood.

Middle Island, ZIP 11953, is about 5 miles east, with Artist Lake, Pine Lake and Spring Lake and the Carmans River headwaters inside Cathedral Pines County Park. Low spots near those waters can take on groundwater after heavy rain, and 30.1% of households there are single-person, so a leak can run for days before anyone sees it. Ask a neighbor to check on a house when you are away.

Calls also come from Port Jefferson Station, Port Jefferson, Nesconset, Miller Place and Saint James. These places do not have their own pages, so describe the loss in as much detail as you can.

What the 24/7 line covers

The line takes calls at any hour for active leaks, flooding, ceilings that are sagging and sewage backups. Same-day response is offered when crews are available, and no arrival time is promised. The dispatcher asks where the water is coming from, how far it has spread and whether power or gas is involved.

  • The address, nearest main road and cross street
  • Where the water started and whether you have stopped it
  • Which rooms and floors are wet
  • Whether you have a sewer or a private septic system
  • Your insurer, if you plan to file a claim

The line does not repair roofs, replace pipes, fix wiring or waterproof foundations, and the dispatcher will tell you which trade to call. We handle extraction, drying, cleanup and rebuild, with the scope and price explained before work starts.

Questions we hear in Selden

Do you cover every ZIP code in the Selden area?

Dedicated pages cover Selden 11784, Mount Sinai 11766 and Middle Island 11953. Other ZIPs, including Port Jefferson 11777, Nesconset 11767, Miller Place 11764 and Saint James 11780, are nearby, and the dispatcher confirms availability by address.

How do I know if I am in Selden or Coram?

Check the mailing address on your water bill or tax bill. A Selden address uses ZIP 11784, and Coram is the neighbor to the east. If you are unsure, give the cross street and the dispatcher will sort it out.

Is the line open on holidays?

Dispatch runs 24/7, including holidays and weekends. Same-day response is offered when crews are available, and no arrival time is promised.

Do you work on commercial buildings along NY 25?

Yes. Shops, offices and multi-unit buildings follow a plan that limits downtime and records the loss, as described on the commercial water damage page.

Who do I call for a water main problem outside my property?

Your water bill shows who your supplier is. SCWA supplies public water across Suffolk County and posts a main break map at scwa.com/main-breaks. Inside the house, shut off the main valve and call us for the cleanup.
