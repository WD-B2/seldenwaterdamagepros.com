Emergency Water Heater Leak Cleanup in Selden, NY

Cut the heater's power and water, then let a crew clear and dry what the tank released.

Call (934) 223-1875

A leaking tank can empty 40 to 80 gallons onto your floor, so act before you assess. Turn off the heater's power or gas and close its cold water inlet, then call our 24/7 line at (934) 223-1875 and keep clear of the puddle.

Water heater leak cleanup in Selden, NY is the removal of water and the drying of floors, walls and contents after a tank, fitting or relief valve leaks. The water is usually warm and clean, which gives crews a good window to save materials.

What to do right now when the tank leaks

  1. Electric heater: switch off its breaker if the panel is dry and safe to reach.
  2. Gas heater: turn the gas control to off. If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911 or the gas utility first.
  3. Close the cold water inlet valve on top of the tank.
  4. Open a hot tap upstairs to relieve pressure and drain the lines.
  5. Do not touch wet wiring, and stay out of standing water near the heater.

Sediment buildup and a worn anode rod are the usual reasons tanks fail from the inside. A relief valve that keeps discharging is a safety warning, not a nuisance, so have it checked before the tank is used again.

Where the water goes in an older house

Postwar houses in Selden, built in the surge of suburban development from the early 1950s, often put the heater in a basement or utility closet. If that basement is finished, the water spreads under carpet, into the base of drywall and across the subfloor before you notice it. That makes quick extraction and the work described on basement water damage matter more than the size of the puddle suggests.

Heaters in attics or upper floor closets work differently, because water drops through the ceiling below. In that case read about ceiling water damage repair as well.

How crews clean up a heater flood

The first step is extraction with a submersible pump or wet vacuum, followed by lifting any wet carpet pad. Moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera then map the drywall, baseboard and subfloor, since heater water tends to wick up walls several inches above the visible line.

Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry the rest. If you want the water removal process in detail, see our water extraction page. For longer drying jobs, our structural drying page explains monitoring.

Replace it, dry it, or both

Flooring

Tile and sealed vinyl usually dry fine. Laminate swells at the seams and often has to be replaced.

Carpet and pad

Warm clean water caught early lets carpet be extracted and dried. The pad generally goes.

Drywall

Bottom courses wicked by heater water can be dried if firm, and cut out if soft or stained.

Stored items

Cardboard boxes, paper and fabric hold water and mold risk. Sort and lift them early.

Who replaces the heater

We are a water damage restoration company, not a plumbing company. A licensed plumber replaces the tank, and for gas units may also need to reconnect the line and vent. Permit rules vary, so ask the Town of Brookhaven Division of Building whether your replacement needs one.

If you can, have the plumber wait to close up the area until we have taken moisture readings. Dry floors and walls can be confirmed more easily while the area is open.

What shapes the bill

  • Water category, which is typically clean for a tank leak.
  • Square footage reached and whether the floor is finished.
  • Materials, from tile to laminate to plaster.
  • Number of days drying equipment stays on site.
  • Rebuild scope after drying.

Call us for a scope and price explained before work starts, and ask about same-day response if the leak is active. Dispatch also reaches Middle Island, about 5 miles east.

Insurance and appliance leaks

A tank that fails suddenly is often a covered loss, while a slow drip that went unnoticed for months often is not. Check your policy, and take photos before cleanup. We record moisture readings and write a scope that you can submit with your own claim.

Safety while you wait

Hot water and wiring

Water from a heater can be hot enough to scald. Stay out of the pool around the unit, and if the heater sits in standing water, do not touch it or its wiring. Cut power only if the panel is dry and safe to reach, and call 911 or the gas utility first if you smell gas.

Questions we hear in Selden

Do you handle water heater leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Selden?

Yes. Dispatch is open 24/7, and we aim for same-day response when crews are available, including after hours. We do not promise an arrival time, but we help you through shut-off steps first.

Should I drain the tank?

After power and gas are off, a plumber can drain it. Water is hot, so do not attach a hose and drain it without protective gloves and a safe drain point.

Is the water from my heater clean?

Usually yes, so it is treated as Category 1. If it has stood a day or two or mixed with floor contents, it may be reclassified.

Do you replace the water heater?

No. A licensed plumber does that. We clear the water, dry the structure and document the damage.

Will the leak cause mold?

Mold needs moisture and organic material, so wet drywall or boxes can support it. Dry materials within 24 to 48 hours, and see mold remediation if it appears.

How soon can the area be dry?

Small areas dry in a few days. Meter readings tell us when materials are back to a dry reference.
