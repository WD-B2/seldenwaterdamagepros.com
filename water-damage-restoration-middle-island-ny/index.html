The hamlet sits about midway between the island's ends and shores, at 82 feet of elevation. Elevation alone does not say whether a particular lot stays dry, so check your address on the FEMA flood map and ask the Town of Brookhaven what it knows about your parcel. The sections below begin with the lakes, then the age of the houses around them.

Lakes, ponds and the Carmans River headwaters

Middle Island has Artist Lake, Pine Lake and Spring Lake, and the Carmans River headwaters now begin inside Cathedral Pines County Park. A home close to any of these may sit lower than the road, so heavy rain can lift ground water into a basement or the soil under a crawl space. If you live near the water, learn where your sump discharge goes and keep stored items off basement floors.

In August 2014 more than 13 inches of rain fell at Islip in about a day, and Suffolk County and the Town of Brookhaven declared a state of emergency. That gauge was not in Middle Island, but it shows how fast a single storm can outrun drainage. When water has already come in, see basement water damage cleanup or crawl space water damage for how those two spaces are dried.

Older lake-community houses and what is inside the walls

Settlement here goes back to at least 1766, and Estonian refugees settled mainly around Pine Lake in the 1940s and 1950s. Smaller houses from those years, and the additions made to them since, can still carry old supply lines and mixed plumbing materials. A corroded joint in that kind of system is one cause of a burst pipe that floods a kitchen or bath.

A tank that has aged in place is another source, and a water heater leak often goes unseen behind a closet door. If your house predates 1980, ask about testing before anyone removes old flooring, plaster or wall coverings, since some older materials need it before demolition.

Before we arrive in Middle Island

Stop the source or shut the main valve

In older lake-community houses the main may be in a crawl space or utility closet. Find it before you need it.

Power off to wet areas only if safe

Switch off only wet rooms, and only from a dry panel. Wet basement panels mean call the utility.

Lift valuables and furniture

Stand sofas on foil or blocks, lift rugs, and move electronics to a dry floor.

Keep people and pets out of contaminated water

Backed-up drains or ground water seeping in are not clean. Keep children and pets in dry rooms.

When water is spreading in Middle Island

Picture a bathroom supply line that lets go at night. By morning the hall carpet is squishing, the ceiling in the room below is bowed, and the baseboard has darkened. That is water spreading, and the next 24 to 48 hours matter most for drying.

  • Close the fixture valve or the main shutoff.
  • Shut off breakers to the wet rooms only when you can reach a dry panel safely.
  • Take photos and short video before moving anything.
  • Call for extraction, then open doors and closets to air.

Our crews use extraction units to remove standing water, then air movers and LGR dehumidifiers to dry the rest. Gas smell or sparking means leave and call 911 or the utility before anything else.

When one person lives in the house

About 30.1% of Middle Island households are single-person, per the 2020 Census, and 4.6% of homes were vacant. A leak in a home with one occupant, or none, can run for days before anyone sees it. Give a trusted neighbor or relative a key and the shutoff location, and ask them to look in after storms or a hard freeze.

Water that ran for weeks soaks insulation and subfloor, so it calls for structural drying with moisture mapping, not just a fan. If mold has already appeared, mold remediation must follow the repair of the leak. In New York, mold assessors and remediators need state licences and may not do both on one property, so ask to see the licence.

Questions we hear in Selden

Is emergency water removal available in Middle Island?

Yes. We dispatch around the clock, including holidays and weekends, and offer same-day response when crews are available. No arrival time is promised on the phone.

Until we arrive, stop the water at its source and keep wet-area outlets off if the panel is safe to reach.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Middle Island?

Middle Island has one ZIP code, 11953, and we serve it. Tell us whether you are off NY 25, Old Middle Island Road, Middle Island-Miller Place Road or Yaphank-Middle Island Road, so we can plan access.

Miller Place (11764) is a nearby ZIP, not one of ours. Call and we will say plainly whether we can reach you.

My neighbor is away and I see water. What should I do?

If it is safe, look for the source from outside and call the owner. Do not enter a flooded house with live power. Call us and we can talk through next steps with the owner.

Does the lake or river affect my coverage?

Regular homeowners insurance usually excludes flood, and flood has its own policy. Ask your insurer, and check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your address.

Do you replace plumbing?

No. We are a restoration company, not a plumber. Call a licensed plumber for the repair, and we will handle drying and rebuild.
