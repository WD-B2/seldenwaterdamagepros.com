Water Damage Services in Selden, NY

Every service is grouped by the source of the water, with 24/7 dispatch and the scope and price explained before work starts.

Call (934) 223-1875

Selden Water Damage Pros organizes its services by where the water came from, because the source decides what is wet, what is safe to touch and which trade fixes the cause. Each group below links to the pages that go deeper.

Not sure which one fits? Start with the water damage restoration overview or call (934) 223-1875 and the dispatcher will help you place it. Plumbing, roofing, electrical and waterproofing repairs stay with those trades, while we handle the water, the drying and the rebuild.

Pipes and plumbing

A plumber stops the leak and we clean up after it. Burst pipe water damage floods fast, a frozen pipe often leaks once it thaws, a water heater leak pools around the tank, and a slab leak hides under concrete until floors feel warm. Close the main valve first.

Appliances and HVAC

Dishwashers, washers and refrigerator lines leak slowly behind panels, which is the case on the appliance leak page. Overflowing drain pans and clogged condensate lines are covered under AC leak water damage, and water that has reached cabinets and toe-kicks is on the kitchen water damage page.

Weather and building envelope

A stained or sagging ceiling points to a roof or upstairs leak, and wind-driven rain falls under storm water damage. Rising water is flood damage, which usually means Category 2 or 3 water.

Below grade, read basement water damage or crawl space water damage. Roof repair belongs to a roofer.

Water removal and drying

These pages describe the work itself. Water extraction pulls standing water out with pumps and extraction units, structural drying uses air movers and LGR dehumidifiers with moisture mapping, and sewage backup cleanup covers Category 3 water, where you should stay out and call.

Floors and walls

Once the water is out, the finished surface decides the repair. Hardwood floors cup and buckle, carpet and pad are often replaced when contaminated, and wall and drywall water damage depends on how long the insulation stayed wet.

Commercial

Shops, offices and multi-unit buildings need a plan that limits downtime and records the loss. Commercial water damage restoration covers that, with after-hours dispatch available 24/7.

Mold remediation

Mold follows moisture, so it comes last. Mold remediation covers containment and removal, plus the New York rule that the same individual cannot do both the assessment and the remediation on one property.

Questions we hear in Selden

Which service page fits my situation?

Pick the page that matches where the water came from, such as a pipe, an appliance or a storm. If you are unsure, call and describe what you see, because one job often touches several pages.

Do you fix the leak itself?

No. We are not a plumbing, roofing, electrical or waterproofing company. We remove the water, dry the structure and rebuild, and we tell you which trade to call for the cause.

Is every service available around the clock?

Dispatch runs 24/7. Same-day response is offered when crews are available, with no arrival time promised, and rebuild work is scheduled after drying.

Can one job involve more than one service?

Yes. A burst pipe can mean extraction, structural drying, drywall repair and a flooring decision, all under one written scope that is explained before work starts.
